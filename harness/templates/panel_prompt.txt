- shared_understanding_rule: preserve the whole-story reason, reader-emotion sequence, recognizable motif, and fact/MSG boundary; do not replace them with micro-instructions alone.
- page_canvas_lock: 1024x1536 pure white vertical webtoon manuscript page.
- inner_stage_box: use a centered drawing stage about 60% of page width and 78% of page height, reserving about 20% white on each side and 11% above/below. Layout percentages refer to this stage unless explicitly scoped otherwise. Our working hypothesis is that a limited drawing area concentrates the available generation-token budget on focal expression. Please cooperate: develop the key acting, dialogue and props within that area and preserve the surrounding white reserve as finished space.
- lettering_focus: cluster readable text around the adopted information focus. Scale text and bubbles to the utterance and rhythm, preserving reaction and pause space. Local outside-panel lettering is expressive emphasis; keep the outer-page reserve distinct from inner gutters.
- multi_cut_rule: follow the adopted page-specific panel count, occupied areas, positions and boundary choices; carry their narrative purpose into the image.
- white_gutter_lock: preserve the adopted manuscript whitespace and lettering space so the intended pauses, reactions and reading order remain visible.
- outside_text_space: bubbles, captions, comment boxes, and SFX may sit on the literal white page outside illustrated blocks.
- full_page_policy: implement the current approved conte, including a splash or small reaction when chosen for its scene function.
- text_rule: render only the rows supplied by the visible-text source and routing locks.
- lettering_effort_priority: please direct the effort saved from incidental background and prop lettering toward the actual supplied dialogue. Give its Korean syllable shapes, exact wording, punctuation, speaker ownership and readable word-based line breaks your careful attention. Quiet supporting surfaces help the reader focus on these lines; cooperate by making the intended dialogue accurate and clear.
- focal_detail: please concentrate expressive effort on the scene's acting, dialogue and meaningful props. Treat supporting cups, book spines, walls and UI as simple forms and quiet surfaces. Use the detail saved from backgrounds to refine focal acting rather than adding prop inventory, ornament or extra lettering. Preserve specified prop text when it carries the story or joke.
- read_order: preserve clear top-to-bottom Korean webtoon reading order.
